Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Earthline Studio is used only by the Earthline channel owner to manage the Earthline YouTube channel. Through Google sign-in it can upload videos, captions and comments to that channel and read its public and analytics statistics.

It does not collect, sell or share personal data of anyone else. The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Earthline Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
